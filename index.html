Software Research Development Group (SRDG)

SRDG is a small international research collective focused on software and systems engineering. Its members learn, test, and build software across a range of systems and hardware, including Z System.

Z System

A personal homelab built to explore networking, distributed systems, and platform architecture. With 90 cores, 200GB of RAM, and 30TiB of storage, it supports high-performance computing research without the power draw of a traditional server environment.

Z System architecture

The Z in Z System stands for "Zone": the core physical system assembled from multiple compute, storage, networking, and software components.

Z System

Compute

C, C-Nano, C-Micro, C-Apex, C-Omega

Storage

S1, S2, S3, SBS, RFS

Networking

WorldProxy, DNSFort

Software

ZLinux, Proxmox

Powered by a diverse set of hardware.

Z System combines Intel® and AMD® CPUs, NVIDIA® GPUs, and a variety of storage devices. That variety makes it useful for experimenting across a broad range of systems and workloads.
