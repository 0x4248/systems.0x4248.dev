About FireFly

FireFly is a tool to create large datasets of text heavy websites and pdfs to create a private and self hosted inteligence dataset that can be used to train AI models or make local intelligence agents without relying on the internet.

Main Features

  • Quality control and content firewall
  • Data sorting and file indexing
  • DuckDuckGo and SearXNG integration for privacy-first web scraping
  • Full hashing and tracking of downloaded content
  • Autonomous research and fixed topic research modes
