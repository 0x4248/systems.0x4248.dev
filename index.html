About 4248 Systems View projects

A personal initiative, run like an enterprise.

4248 Systems is my personal sub-brand of 0x4248, focused on systems engineering and research relating to networking and computer data systems.

It isn't a company, charity or non-profit, but rather a personal initiative to explore networks and distributed systems. The goal is to build an enterprise-grade homelab and learn how real world systems are built and operated.

Software Research Development Group (SRDG)

SRDG is an private international research collective focused on software and systems engineering, that works with hardware such as Z System. The group is composed of several people from around the world, learning, testing and building software.

This also isn't a company or non-profit.
