About 4248 Systems

4248 Systems is my personal sub-brand of 0x4248, focused on systems engineering and research relating to networking and computer data systems.
4248 Systems isnt a company, charity or non profit, but rather a personal initiative to explore and research the field of networks and distributed systems. The goal of 4248 Systems is to build an enterprise grade homelab and learn how the real world systems are built, and how they operate.

Powered by a diverse set of hardware.

4248 DATASYSTEM

The 4248 DataSystem is what I have called my homelab which contains 6 servers, 2 network switches, and 2 routers. The DataSystem uses 3 recycled office computers and are upgraded and repurposed to be used as servers. The other 3 servers are a cluster of low power raspberry Pi 4s and 5s which are used for testing and low power workloads. DataSystem is designed from thr ground up to be low power and reliable.
